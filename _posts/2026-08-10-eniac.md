Our paper *Benchmarking Foundation Models for Zero-Shot Water Demand Forecasting* has been accepted at the Encontro Nacional de Inteligência Artificial e Computacional (ENIAC), which will be held alongside (BRACIS)](https://bracis.sbc.org.br/2026/) in Cuiabá, Mato Grosso, Brazil, from October 19 to 22. 

This publication is the result of the undergraduate thesis by Luana Rathunde Sandler, lead author of the work.

The study evaluates the zero-shot application of time series foundation models, specifically Chronos and Moirai, for daily water demand forecasting. Using data from a coastal city in Southern Brazil characterized by high seasonal volatility due to tourism, we compared these approaches against established statistical and machine learning baselines. Our findings demonstrate that Transformer-based foundation models achieve competitive performance, highlighting their viability for rapid deployment or data-scarce scenarios.
